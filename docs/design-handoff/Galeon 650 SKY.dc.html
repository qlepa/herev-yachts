WHO IT'S FOR

Your first big yacht, without the guesswork.

The 650 SKY is the Galeon most families step up to first — a proper three-deck flybridge that still handles like a smaller boat. The skydeck doubles your living space in the sun; below, four cabins mean everyone aboard has a door that closes.

If you're moving up from a weekender and want space to grow into — for guests, for longer passages, for the years ahead — this is the sensible, generous choice.
