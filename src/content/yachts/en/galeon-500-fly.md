## Two yachts in one.

**The 500 FLY combines the sleek profile of a hardtop cruiser with the practicality of a flybridge yacht. The panoramic helm deck doubles your living space in the sun; below, three owner-grade cabins mean everyone aboard has a door that closes.**

If you're moving up from a weekender and want space to grow into — for guests, for longer passages, for the years ahead — this is the sensible, generous choice.

- Panoramic flybridge with second helm
- Fold-out Beach Mode balconies
- Three en-suite cabins
- Volvo IPS joystick docking
- Award-winning Galeon design
